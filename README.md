# A Child, a UFO, and the Village on the Hill — coolest-art-01 (v6 entry)

Entry for the make.fun bounty coolest-art-01: a small child stands in a
village on a hill at night and looks up as a little green alien descends
inside a beam of light from a huge glowing UFO hovering in front of
them. Drawn procedurally by `main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library
only) — no vendored image; every pixel is generated by the program's own
drawing code. The scene is the user's own idea, keeping the judge-liked
pattern (a tiny human against a huge cosmic thing, flat navy/gold/red
style) while being clearly different from the competition's top-1 image
and from our own v5.
